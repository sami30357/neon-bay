# Neon Bay

Neon Bay is a sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game set in Solmara Bay — a bright-day bay city with a real waterfront: a beach, Pier 7 and the open sea. Drive and walk the bright streets, steal cars, outrun the Bay PD and its searchlight helicopter, and work eleven story jobs for fixer Vex Marlowe. The city runs on a real clock — night, dawn, day, golden hour and dusk — and it opens at bright noon, 12:00, the whole bay glittering. v8 adds the waterfront, a crew of three with abilities (Mira swaps the plates, Zio patches you up, Kiki spots the law), named police units with roles, district labels, and Job 11 TIDE RUNNER down the boardwalk. Built for make.fun's [Make GTA 6](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) bounty.

## What is in the game

- **A bright-noon opening**: the game clock starts at 12:00 and runs 1 game-hour per 75 real seconds. Bright noon is the first thing you see — sun on the water, palms and umbrellas on the sand, Pier 7 reaching into the bay — then golden hour, dusk goes purple, night brings the rain and the full neon, dawn breaks pale over the bay. Sky, fog, lights, sun and moon, rain, window glow and streetlights all follow the hour, and the HUD shows the time and phase. `?tod=<hours>` pins the time for screenshots.
- **A real waterfront**: the south edge of the city is Solmara Bay — a scrolling water plane with a pulsing foam line, a sand beach with striped umbrellas, towels and beachgoers, palms along the boardwalk, Pier 7 (deck, posts, rails, shack, sign) reaching 88 units out over the water, four boats bobbing in the bay and green islands on the horizon. Nothing walks or drives past the waterline; the helicopter can fly out over the bay but never steps out over deep water.
- **A night city**: a seeded street grid of towers with lit windows, landmark towers with helipads and blinking beacons, neon signs (some flicker), billboards, street props, a starry sky with moon and drifting clouds, wet reflective asphalt, rain streaks, ground splashes and puddles.
- **On foot and by car**: third-person walking with mouse-look, sprint lean and a visible pistol, and arcade driving with handbrake drift, horn and brake lights.
- **Traffic and pedestrians**: cars cruise the lanes — including yellow cabs and vans — pedestrians stroll the sidewalks with hats and umbrellas and flee when trouble starts.
- **Steal cars**: walk up to any parked car and press E. Stealing raises your wanted level. Stealing a car *in traffic* is a carjacking: the driver gets pulled out, flees on foot, the camera shakes — and the car is yours a beat later.
- **Flyable helicopter**: the gold-and-white SKYHAWK sits on a ground helipad by the marina. Press E beside it to climb in — W/S thrust, A/D yaw, Space climb, Shift descend, up to ~108 km/h. It slides over rooftops instead of clipping through them, can fly out over the bay, and land on the pad (or anywhere low) and press E to step out — never over deep water.
- **Street races**: three checkpoint races at the checkered flag on the pier — PIER SPRINT (4 rings, par 1:15, $400), NEON LOOP (6 rings, par 2:00, $700), BAY GRAND PRIX (8 rings, par 3:00, $1,000). Press E at the flag when no job is running. Beat the par for the full payout, over par pays half; best times save with your game.
- **Wanted level and police**: 5 stars of heat. Police cars chase and ram with bright flashing light bars and red/blue road pools; foot cops chase and grab you; at 4 stars a police helicopter joins with a searchlight and rotor thump — and the PD throws a roadblock across the road ahead of you, two stationary police cars, lights flashing. Ram one at speed and it hurts. Every unit carries a real callsign (TARPON 38, KITE-1…) and a role — RAMMING, FLANKING, SPOTLIGHT, ROADBLOCK — shown in the Bay PD response list with distances. Lose them to cool down; get busted or wasted and you pay for it.
- **Missions and story**: eleven jobs from Vex — a delivery run, a car theft, a back-alley fight, a blackout run under police heat, a tower finale, a night driving a cab, a five-star coronation run, a dawn getaway stealing the gold Sunrise GT from the marina, a marina run under four-star heat, a golden-hour finale stealing the Celeste Crown jewel from Meridian Tower under five stars, and a boardwalk run for the tide crate at Pier 7. Each pays cash and unlocks the next.
- **Crew**: three faces with three abilities — Mira the driver swaps the plates (Z, lose a wanted star), Zio the mechanic patches you up (X, +50 HP), Kiki the lookout spots every PD unit for 10 seconds (C). Each has a cooldown shown in the crew panel.
- **Garage upgrades**: Garage 9 tunes your ride — Engine (top speed + accel), Armor (damage taken −15% per level) and Handling (sharper steering), 3 levels each, $400 / $800 / $1,400. Press G at the garage. Garage 9 is also your safehouse: press E there to save and heal to full.
- **Weapon wheel**: pistol (infinite ammo), SMG (fast fire) and shotgun (wide spread, heavy hits). Keys 1 / 2 / 3 or Q to switch; the HUD shows your weapon and ammo.
- **Shop**: AMMO-NATION on Volt Row sells medkits ($100, full heal), SMG ammo ($150 for 120 rounds) and shotgun shells ($150 for 24). Press E at the shop.
- **Neon orbs**: 12 glowing orbs hidden around the city — collect them for $50 each. They show on the minimap.
- **Combat**: punch up close, shoot with the mouse. Enemies fight back. Hard hits shake the screen, flash the edges and puff crash smoke.
- **HUD with a live minimap**: cash, health, wanted stars, weapon + ammo, speed, mission objective, orb count, respect rank (ROOKIE → SHOT CALLER), crew cooldowns, the Bay PD response list, and a top-down map with the player, traffic, police, helicopter, roadblocks and mission marker — plus a district label (SOLMARA STRAND, PIER 7, MARINA, VOLT ROW, NEON ROW, MERIDIAN CROSSING) showing where you are.
- **Start screen, pause menu and saves**: progress saves after every job; continue where you left off.
- **Synthesized sound**: engine hum, sirens, helicopter rotor, horn, gunshots, rain — all made with WebAudio, no audio files.

## Controls

- **W A S D** move / drive · **Mouse** look · **Shift** sprint / heli descend · **E** enter / steal car / heli / shop / safehouse / race · **F** punch · **Click** shoot · **1 2 3** pistol / SMG / shotgun · **Q** cycle weapon · **G** garage upgrades · **Space** handbrake / heli climb · **R** reset car · **Enter** accept job · **M** mute · **Esc** pause

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. The game is plain static files under `game/` and plays offline — every script, texture and sound is generated in the browser or vendored under `game/`.

## Tech

- Three.js (vendored minified build under `game/vendor/`) renders the 3D night city.
- `main.ts` copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `dossier.json` with the kit's `vendor/makefun-game.ts`.
